# Privacy policy

KeyOTP collects no personal data and contains no analytics or tracking.

- OATH accounts are stored on your YubiKey, not on the device or on any server.
- YubiKey passwords are only saved, in the device keychain, if you choose to save them.
- When you sync from Ente Auth, the app talks only to Ente's server (or the server you enter). Your Ente
  password never leaves the device, and your accounts are decrypted on the device. If you choose to stay signed
  in, the Ente session is kept in the device keychain, protected by Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Ente's
  own privacy policy applies to your Ente account.
- The camera is only used to scan QR codes, and NFC only to talk to your YubiKey.

Questions: https://github.com/SINF-KEN/yubioath-ios/issues
